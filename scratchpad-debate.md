# TFIS Redesign Scratchpad Debate

## Participants
- **DeepSeek Evaluator** (mytoken provider, DeepSeek-V4-Flash model)
- **Qwen Evaluator** (mytoken provider, Qwen3.6-27B model)
- **Gemma Evaluator** (mytoken provider, gemma-4-31B-it model)

## Debate Rules
1. Each evaluator submits their critique of the current layout
2. Each evaluator then reviews the other two critiques and pushes back
3. All three converge on a unified redesign direction
4. Final output: agreed content hierarchy + visual direction + UX funnel

## Points for Debate / Pushback

The following tensions need resolution:

### Tension 1: Content ordering
- Where does "The Meaning of Life" essay go? It's philosophical depth — does it belong at the bottom as deep reading, or should it appear earlier as the "why"?
- Where does the 50-Philosopher appendix go? Collapse it? Replace with a searchable list?

### Tension 2: SSA self-assessment → CTA flow
- Current site has no self-assessment widget. The brief says CTA should differentiate between L0/L1 (course) and L2+ (whitepaper).
- Should we embed a quick 5-question mini-assessment in the hero? Or at the SSA ladder section?
- How to nudge without being pushy?

### Tension 3: The WITMOL essay's role
- Is it a core tenant that should be prominent, or supplementary reading?
- Should the 6 Axioms from The Functional Life merge directly into the 5 Pillars?

### Tension 4: Visual direction within ultra-minimalist constraint
- "White mode only" is strict. How do we communicate "solo" feeling? More negative space? Monospace-heavy? Different typography?
- Should we use a serif font for body (like the original WITMOL did with Georgia) vs the current Inter?

### Tension 5: Navigation
- Flat single-page vs multi-section with a persistent table of contents?
- The current 3-tab nav (Framework, Functional Life, Meaning of Life) is too broad for the amount of content.

### Tension 6: CTA placement strategy
- Whitepaper download at the SSA ladder section only? Or also in hero and footer?
- Course CTA should appear for L0/L1 visitors — but how do we know their level without assessment?

## Resolution Format
After all pushback rounds, output a single agreed:
1. Content hierarchy (ordered section list)
2. UX funnel diagram (text)
3. Visual direction statement
4. CTA placement map
