## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How can I contribute to your research?**
A: We welcome case studies, data sharing, interview participation, and collaborative research projects. Contact us at insights@tfis.org with your background and areas of expertise.

**Q: Do you provide consulting services?**
A: TFIS is primarily a research organization. We occasionally engage in advisory roles for significant implementations, but our focus is on knowledge creation and sharing.

**Q: How do you ensure confidentiality for sensitive business information?**
A: We have strict protocols for anonymizing data and protecting proprietary information. All case studies are reviewed and approved by participants before publication.

**Q: Can you help with AI implementation?**
A: While we don't provide direct implementation services, our research includes practical frameworks and best practices. We can connect you with practitioners who have successfully implemented similar solutions.

**Q: How often do you publish new research?**
A: We release major reports quarterly, with ongoing insights shared through our newsletter and social media channels.

## Research Ethics & Standards

TFIS adheres to rigorous research standards:
- Independent verification of claims where possible
- Transparent methodology disclosure
- Proper attribution and citations
- Respect for participant confidentiality
- Balanced presentation of benefits and risks
