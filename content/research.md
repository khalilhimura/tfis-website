# Research & Findings

## Current Research Focus

Our research explores three interconnected areas that are reshaping how business value is created and captured in the age of AI.

### 1. The Rise of AI-Powered Solopreneurship

**Key Finding**: Individual entrepreneurs can now handle enterprise-level complexity using AI agents for coding, marketing, operations, and customer service.

**Research Highlights**:
- Solo founders building MVPs in weeks, not months
- AI agents handling 80%+ of routine business operations
- Customer acquisition costs dropping 60% through AI-optimized campaigns
- Single-person companies managing thousands of clients simultaneously

### 2. Enterprise Agentic Transformation

**Key Finding**: Large organizations are adopting multi-agent systems that plan, execute, and improve tasks autonomously.

**Current Data Points**:
- Gartner forecasts 33% of enterprise software will include agentic AI by 2028
- 15% of daily business decisions being automated
- Organizational hierarchies compressing by 2-3 levels on average
- OpenAI's 5-level agentic hierarchy becoming industry standard

### 3. Democratization of AI-Driven Leverage

**Key Finding**: Cloud platforms and open-source models have dramatically reduced the cost and complexity of deploying advanced AI capabilities.

**Market Analysis**:
- 90% reduction in AI deployment costs since 2020
- Open-source models achieving 95% of proprietary model performance
- Self-improving reasoning systems emerging across multiple domains
- Collaborative agent frameworks enabling seamless task handoffs

## Emerging Trends

### The One-Person Unicorn Race
Multiple industry leaders predict the emergence of billion-dollar single-founder companies:
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman: "Ten-person companies with billion-dollar valuations"
- Tim Cortinovis (Single-Handed Unicorn): Strategic problem identification over headcount growth
- Industry consensus shifting from "if" to "who will be first"

### Agentic Platform Evolution
- **Level 1-2 Agents**: Task execution and basic reasoning
- **Level 3-4 Agents**: Complex planning and coordination  
- **Level 5 Agents**: Strategic decision-making partners

### Economic Disruption Patterns
- Traditional SMEs facing pressure from AI-enhanced competitors
- New job categories emerging: AI oversight, ethical auditing, human-centric services
- Reskilling requirements accelerating across all sectors
