Self-Assessment Protocol

This assessment uses an adaptive staircase to estimate your self-reported SSA-CMM practice level across five pillars: Agency, Clarity, Competence, Accountability, and Security.

You'll answer 15 items (3 per pillar). Each item adapts based on your previous responses. The assessment takes approximately 10 minutes.
