Book & reader updates

Changelog

Updates to the book and its online reader, newest first.

September 11, 2026

Your place, saved

Optional email login now lets you keep your reading progress across devices. Mark chapters complete and return to where you left off from My library.

Save highlighted passages with your own comments, thoughts, and links. Your private library lets you search your notes, filter by source, and jump back to a passage. The book remains free to read without an account.

A clearer way into the book

The opening page now includes a book abstract and a guided reading journey. The new executive summary brings together the main thesis, the Mind → Memory → System architecture, the SSA-CMM ladder, and links to the practical work in each chapter.

Reading improvements

  • A new Changelog page keeps book and reader updates together, with a link after Reference in the book contents.

  • Tables wrap text and size their columns automatically for easier reading.

  • External references open in a new tab, keeping your place in the book.

  • The fictional practice case now follows Audy and Himura Inc.

September 10, 2026 — First edition

The first edition brings together 24 chapters across Mind, Memory, and System, with three appendices, seven original diagrams, and 40 practical field-kit entries.

The online reader includes chapter navigation, search, light and dark themes, and a print view. You can also download the manuscript and source bundle to keep the book and its exercises with you.
