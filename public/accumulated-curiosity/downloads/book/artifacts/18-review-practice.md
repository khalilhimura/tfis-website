## Worked interpretation

The exercise brief and revised action list are different objects. Thursday belongs only to the exercise brief in L1. L4 and L5 establish a separate action and shared owner, with Tuesday/Wednesday deadlines that conflict. The packet supplies no supersession. Combining them as “materials” changes the relationships and conceals uncertainty.

The first row must be rewritten. Instructions-v3 already requires source fidelity and unresolved conflict handling. No new instruction version is warranted by this violation.
